Architecture firm

Studio Carney

  • Original. A portfolio site with staff sign-in, project uploads, and a content editor. The home page was a full-screen stock photo and the firm name, with no work in sight.
  • Redesign. An editorial layout in a light serif that leads with the firm's own projects. A numbered project index, the firm's existing and proposed plans side by side, and 21 services grouped into plan, design, and build show how the firm works before anyone calls.

Day spa

Get Pampered

  • Original. A spa site with tabbed price menus and structured data for local search. The home page was mostly long product descriptions, and booking meant finding the contact page.
  • Redesign. A calm, premium look with arched photos, a booking bar near the top, and the whole menu on one page with every price from the original site. The spa's own Relax, Rejuvenate, Revive line ties the sections together.

Research prototype, Misfit Lab

Alt text for BTS ARMY

  • Original. A Ruby on Rails prototype for UC Santa Cruz's Misfit Lab. BTS ARMY fans could sign in, upload an image, write a description, credit the source, add tags, and mark it verified. It used default, unstyled forms.
  • Redesign. A focused writing screen. The image sits beside the description field, a preview shows how a screen reader will read it aloud, a checklist covers good alt text, and a review queue shows which descriptions other fans have verified.

Result

Two client websites and a research prototype, designed and built end to end, and redesigned for desktop and mobile.
