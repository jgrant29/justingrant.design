About

I'm a UX designer who starts with research and stays through launch.

I began in sales at hrsimple.com and spent eight years moving into design, building the product myself. Since then I've designed and researched at McGraw Hill, Mozilla, and JPMorganChase.

I hold an M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction from UC Santa Cruz and a B.A. in Philosophy from UCLA.

Outside work, I mentor HCI graduate students at UC Santa Cruz, volunteer on Earth science field campaigns, and grow vegetables.
